Artist Statement


When coming up with my idea for how I would incorporate the randomization of color, I took inspiration from the way leaves change in the fall; some of them remain the same color while others change colors at varying rates. I chose to add randomization to the scale of the circles so that a hypnotic pulsing effect would start to appear, working in tandem with the randomization of color. As far as the pattern itself, I’ve always been drawn to Aztec and Art Deco line work, so I tried to incorporate a mix between the two. To me, it reminds me of a big, bright sign with flashing lights that you would see in the 40s era.
